# Haonan Li

Undergraduate majoring in Statistics at Capital University of Economics and Business and Data Science at the University of Arizona. I work across statistical modeling, machine learning, and data systems, with projects in biomedical and health data, computational biology, financial and business analytics, decision analysis and systems engineering, and applied AI products.

## Research, publications, and innovation

- [DNA Methylation Bayesian Structure Learning](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/dna-methylation/) - scored 25 three-node DAGs across 121 ENCODE datasets and studied local structural agreement and methylation-state dynamics.
- [Chronic Care Pathway Risk Modeling](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/chronic-care/) - converted longitudinal encounter histories into care pathways, patient segments, and an interpretable acute-event risk model.
- [Personal Credit Default Risk Modeling](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/credit-risk/) - compared five classification models on an imbalanced credit-risk dataset and published the study as sole author.
- [Small-Sample A/B Testing System](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/ab-testing/) - designed a dual-stream adaptive-allocation and randomized-validation framework and became first-named inventor on its published patent application.
- [Xiangchan AI Operations Platform](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/xiangchan/) - built a traceable, human-reviewed workflow for rural-product operations with structured intake, retrieval, generation, and offline fallback.
- [SafeTrace Asset Security](https://haonan-li-source.github.io/lihaonan.github.io/projects/safetrace/) - developed a scenario-based asset-risk and response prototype.
