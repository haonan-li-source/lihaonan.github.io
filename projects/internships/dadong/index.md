# Statistics Bureau of Dadong District, Shenyang

<div class="project-meta"><strong>Role:</strong> Statistical practice intern &nbsp; · &nbsp; <strong>Period:</strong> Jun–Aug 2024 &nbsp; · &nbsp; <strong>Focus:</strong> economic monitoring</div>

## Work context

District statistical monitoring converts enterprise declarations, investment-project records, and supporting evidence into consistent economic indicators. The work requires numerical accuracy together with careful use of statistical definitions, classification standards, and evidence requirements.

## My contribution

- Cleaned and standardized enterprise operating and fixed-asset investment records.
- Used Excel to calculate current-period, cumulative, year-over-year, and month-over-month indicators.
- Flagged zero declarations and unusual changes through cross-period and cross-table checks.
- Assisted with invoice, contract, and equipment-purchase verification.
- Supported monthly and quarterly forecasts using historical series, enterprise information, and project investment plans.
- Decomposed changes by total, incremental contribution, growth, and sector share.
- Reviewed enterprise and project-registration materials, including industry codes, statistical definitions, contract values, and supporting evidence.
- Maintained enterprise directories and project-monitoring ledgers.

## Outputs

Standardized records, anomaly flags, forecast-support tables, enterprise directories, project-monitoring ledgers, and structured inputs for indicator aggregation and reporting.

## Challenge and solution

Large changes may reflect real economic activity, reporting errors, definition differences, or incomplete evidence. I combined numerical checks with document-level verification, comparing current and cumulative values across periods and tables before tracing unusual movements back to enterprise records and investment plans.
