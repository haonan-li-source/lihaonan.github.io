# Investment Research and Data Analysis Internship at Huaneng Investment Management

<div class="project-meta"><strong>Role:</strong> Investment research and data-analysis intern &nbsp; · &nbsp; <strong>Period:</strong> Jul–Sep 2025</div>

<div class="project-actions"><a class="button" href="../">Browse all internship case studies</a></div>

## Business context

The internship combined sector research and quantitative analysis for energy-related investment work. Assignments covered photovoltaics, grid-forming energy storage, humanoid robotics, organizational use of large language models, document retrieval, and A-share IPO pricing and return analysis.

## My contribution

- Reviewed company disclosures and more than ten sell-side reports to build industry frameworks covering supply and demand, technology, cost, competition, and risk.
- Built a photovoltaic supply-demand panel covering 13 regions and a comparison matrix for five representative companies.
- Validated survey skip logic, recoded scale and multiple-choice responses, and grouped open-text themes for an internal LLM-adoption study. I used contingency tables, exploratory chi-square tests, and Cramér's V to examine the relationship between use frequency and perceived efficiency.
- Identified result reliability, multimodal capability, and data recency as major adoption barriers, then translated use cases and reported pain points into a feature-priority matrix for internal intelligent-tool design.
- Co-developed a locally deployed, source-traceable research assistant using Python, Haystack, Whisper, and Ollama for multi-format parsing, retrieval-based Q&A, meeting-note support, and research-draft assistance.
- Built a dataset of 472 A-share IPOs across four boards and 31 industries.
- Calculated opening, closing, VWAP, and allotment-adjusted returns, winsorized extreme values, compared boards with Kruskal-Wallis tests, and used robust regressions with board and industry controls.

## Scale and outputs

Three industry-research tracks, more than ten reports reviewed, a 13-region photovoltaic panel, a five-company matrix, a structured LLM-adoption analysis, a local retrieval prototype, and a 472-IPO analytical dataset.

## Decision relevance and public boundary

The work translated heterogeneous research evidence into three decision formats: comparable sector and company views, prioritized requirements for an internal research tool, and a risk-aware IPO dataset for subscription analysis. Company reports, survey responses, meeting materials, recordings, licensed databases, and internal software packages are not public. This page reports the methods, scales, and decisions without reproducing those proprietary records.

## Challenge and solution

Reports, surveys, and market records used different definitions and data structures. I standardized analytical dimensions, documented recoding rules, cross-checked sources, applied robust statistics to extreme observations, and kept retrieval outputs linked to their supporting documents.
